Vigil of the Mist · Privacy Policy

Updated October 23, 2026

This policy applies to the game Vigil of the Mist (Vigília da Bruma in Portuguese) for Android.

Data the game stores

The game stores your progress (essence, talents, unlocked stages, records and the essence you bought) and your settings (language, sound and music) on your device.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for two things:

  • Leaderboard: your best Endless mode score is sent to the game's Google Play Games leaderboard, where other players can see it next to your Play Games name.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your progress is saved in your Google account (Play Games Saved Games), so it comes back if you reinstall the game or switch phones.

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive it on any server of our own.

Purchases

Essence packs are sold through Google Play billing. Google processes the payment; the game only receives a confirmation of which pack was bought, which it uses to add the essence and keep it in your saved progress. We never see your payment details.

Data the game collects

Nothing beyond the above. The game has no sign-up of its own, shows no ads, uses no analytics and sends no information to any server of ours.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game or clear the app's data in Android settings to delete the progress on your device. To delete the cloud save and leaderboard entries, remove the game's data from your Google Play Games profile (Play Games app, Settings, Delete Play Games account and data, or delete the data for this game only).

Children

The game collects no personal data of its own from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game starts using data in any other way, this page will be updated first, explaining what is used and why.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email on the game's Google Play page.
