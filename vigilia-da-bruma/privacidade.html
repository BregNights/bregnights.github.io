Vigília da Bruma · Privacy Policy

Updated October 5, 2026

This policy applies to the Vigília da Bruma game for Android.

Data the game stores

The game only stores your progress (essence, talents, weapons, unlocked stages) and your settings (language, sound and music). Everything is saved only on your device.

Data the game collects

None. The game has no sign-up, collects no personal data, shows no ads, uses no analytics and sends no information to any server, ours or anyone else's.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game or clear the app's data in Android settings. Your saved progress is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

Children

Since the game collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data, for example for an online leaderboard, this page will be updated first, explaining what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email on the game's Google Play page.
